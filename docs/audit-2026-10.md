# audit — october 2026

a full read of `index.html`, `sw.js` and `manifest.webmanifest`, plus measured
performance profiling. every bug below was traced to a specific code path,
fixed, and now has a regression test in `tests/run.mjs`.

## bugs fixed

| # | severity | bug | fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | high | with site storage blocked (private modes), nothing could play — the safety notice threw | acknowledgement remembered in memory as a fallback |
| 2 | high | a double-tap, remove or reset while a tone was still connecting to a bluetooth device left a tone sounding that nothing could stop | per-tone generation tokens: a stop or newer play cancels a play still waiting on its device |
| 3 | high | moving several playing tones to a device created one audio context per tone; the master volume and limiter missed most of them | one shared context per device (concurrent callers share the pending promise) |
| 4 | high | removing or reordering stages of the playing playlist broke most actions until the stage ended | edits remap the playing stage; removing it stops the playlist cleanly; minute edits move the end time |
| 5 | medium | device audio contexts were never closed | contexts with nothing playing through them (or whose device left) are closed |
| 6 | medium | loading or playing a combo during a playlist was wiped at the next stage | any manual play / load takes over from the playlist |
| 7 | medium | stopping or skipping while a stage was still starting left tones sounding | stage start aborts the moment it's superseded |
| 8 | medium | reset → add → undo threw away the new tone while it kept sounding | undo merges the restored tones with anything added since |
| 9 | medium | toast and dialog hide-timers weren't cancelled (a "use it" confirmation could vanish instantly; a re-opened dialog could disappear) | timers tracked and cleared on open |
| 10 | medium | the pulse caution note never hid again | `[hidden]` now wins over the note's display rule |
| 11 | medium | playlists couldn't be exported without at least one combo | export enabled when either exists |
| 12 | medium | the app's own export could exceed its 256 KB import limit | compact export, 2 MB limit (contents are still capped field by field) |
| 13 | low | a "speakers connected" toast appeared on every page load | the first scan never announces |
| 14 | low | a custom 440 Hz tone marked the library's 440 row as added; clicking it deleted the custom tone | library rows only match tones added from the library |
| 15 | low | a combo that failed to save (storage full) still appeared saved | the item is rolled back on failure |
| 16 | low | dialogs didn't trap or restore focus; the page behind stayed interactive | focus trap, inert background, focus returned to the opener |
| 17 | low | segmented buttons didn't announce their state; card remove buttons were all just "remove" | `aria-pressed` kept in sync; remove labels name the tone |
| 18 | low | on a weak connection the app could hang instead of using the offline copy; "checking offline cache…" could spin forever | 3 s navigation timeout falls back to the cache; status resolves after 15 s |
| 19 | low | device scans could overlap (duplicate toasts and replays) | scans are serialised |
| 20 | — | *(found while testing the new premium layer)* the play button's champagne ring was wiped whenever the icon changed | only the icon is swapped |
| 21 | — | *(found from a screenshot)* text typed straight after ⌘K was lost | focus lands the instant the bar opens |

## performance

see `docs/design-system.md` §5 for the rules and the measurements.
the main cause of lag was the animation loop writing three values onto the page
root every frame, which re-styled the entire document 60 times a second.
