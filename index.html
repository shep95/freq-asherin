extreme caution

these tones can cause pain, nausea or hearing harm to people and animals, and can damage speakers.

    • they start at 20 % volume. keep the master low and sessions brief.
    • never aim them at anyone — people, pets or wildlife. doing so may be unlawful.
    • stop immediately if anyone feels pain, pressure, dizziness or nausea.

    before you listen

    • start quiet. pure tones fatigue hearing faster than music. stop if you hear ringing or feel pressure.
    • beats & pulses. if you have epilepsy or a seizure disorder, ask a doctor before using rhythmic tones. never while driving.
    • not medicine. healing labels are traditional claims, not proven treatments.
    • matter. high levels can stress speakers and rattle objects. high tones can hurt children's and pets' ears.
