# Intelligence Archives on Sound, Acoustics and Audio Frequencies: Declassified Documents and Official Assessments

Source-type labels used throughout: **[A]** primary declassified document; **[B]** official government assessment/report; **[C]** credible journalism/scholarship; **[D]** unverified claim. Confidence labels: **High / Medium / Low**.

Research note: several primary hosts (dni.gov, archive.dni.gov, theblackvault.com, washingtontimes.com) failed DNS resolution from this environment, so some primary PDFs were identified by URL and summarized via search-indexed text and reporting rather than read in full. This is flagged item by item.

## 1. CIA FOIA / MKULTRA-era material on sound, audio, subliminal or sensory techniques

### Takeaway
The surviving MKULTRA record contains little that is specifically acoustic. The two items most often cited are (1) Subproject 54, a Navy-linked proposal for "sub-aural" concussive sound, which the CIA told Congress was never carried out, and (2) Subproject 68 (Ewen Cameron's "psychic driving"), which used repeated recorded verbal messages. Most MKULTRA records were destroyed in 1973, so claims of a large "sonic mind-control" program cannot be documented.

### Cited Findings
- **[A] Subproject 68 (c. 1959):** a CIA draft memo on continuing MKULTRA describes support for research on "the effects upon human behavior of the repetition of verbal signals". This is the documented basis of the auditory "psychic driving" (repeated taped messages) linked to Dr. Ewen Cameron's work in Montreal. Confidence: Medium. The memo was identified through search; I did not read the full OCR. — [CIA Reading Room, "Continuation of MKULTRA" PDF](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CONTINUATION%20OF%20MKULTRA%2C%20%5B10427391%5D.pdf)
- **[B]/[C] Subproject 54 ("Perfect Concussion"):** a proposal, linked to the Navy, to study whether "sub-aural frequency blasts" could erase memory. DCI Stansfield Turner told Congress in 1977 that the project was cancelled and "never carried out." The sound-weapon description comes from the proposal; no result is documented. Confidence: Medium. — [Wikipedia, MKUltra (citing the 1977 Senate hearing)](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/MKULTRA)
- **[A] Subproject files are on the CIA Reading Room** (for example Subproject 102, 16 documents dated 1959–1963; also 136 and 149). The indexed OCR is mostly garbled. Subproject 136 concerns ESP and is not auditory. — [CIA Reading Room, Subproject 102](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/node/2076019); [CIA Reading Room print 2076041](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/print/2076041)
- **[A] The MKSEARCH keyword index** (the successor program) is also available. — [CIA Reading Room keyword: mksearch](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/keyword/mksearch)
- **[C] Record destruction:** most MKULTRA records were destroyed in 1973 on DCI Richard Helms's order. This limits what can be verified about any subproject. — [Wikipedia, MKUltra](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/MKULTRA)
- **[D]** Claims that MKULTRA ran a working subliminal-audio or infrasound mind-control program appear on forums and mirror sites. No CIA document supporting them was found. — search result review (forum mirrors such as m.cafe.daum.net, eventilla) noted in [CIA reading-room search](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/print/982079)

### Inferences
- The documented auditory part of MKULTRA is mainly *verbal repetition* (psychic driving) combined with drugs and sensory deprivation, not frequency-specific "sound weapons." Subproject 54 is a proposal, not evidence of a capability.

### Gaps
- I did not read the full text of the Subproject 54 and Subproject 68 files. A keyword search of the Reading Room PDFs for "subliminal," "auditory" and "sound" is still needed to confirm or exclude other acoustic subprojects.
- The 1977 Senate hearing transcript ("Project MKULTRA, the CIA's Program of Research in Behavioral Modification") was not fetched directly.

## 2. The "Gateway Process" analysis (1983) and Monroe Institute Hemi-Sync / binaural beats

### Takeaway
"Analysis and Assessment of Gateway Process" (dated 9 June 1983, 29 pages) was written by Lt. Col. Wayne M. McDonnell of the US Army Intelligence and Security Command (INSCOM). It is a speculative theoretical write-up, not an experiment. It explains the Monroe Institute's Hemi-Sync method (binaural-beat audio used to "synchronize" the brain's hemispheres) using a mix of physics, holography and consciousness theory. It sits in the CIA's STARGATE collection. No documented operational outcome followed from it. A related Army contract with Monroe for Hemi-Sync training of remote viewers is documented.

### Cited Findings
- **[A] The document:** "Analysis and Assessment of Gateway Process," 9 June 1983, 29 pages, part of the CIA "Stargate Collection." CIA Reading Room ID CIA-RDP96-00788R001700210016-5 (PDF at cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP96-00788R001700210016-5.pdf). — [The Black Vault index entry](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/stargate-collection-analysis-and-assessment-of-gateway-process-9-june-1983/)
- **[A]/[C] Author:** Lt. Col. Wayne M. McDonnell, US Army, writing to his commander at INSCOM. Secondary sources say the CIA released it about 2003. One low-quality site attributes it to a CIA "Technical Intelligence Division"; this conflicts with the INSCOM authorship. Confidence: High on authorship, Medium on the release year. — [The Black Vault](https://theblackvault.com/documentarchive/stargate-collection-analysis-and-assessment-of-gateway-process-9-june-1983/amp)
- **[A]/[C] Content:** the paper covers meditation, hypnosis, biofeedback and Hemi-Sync audio frequencies (binaural beats) meant to bring the two brain hemispheres into coherence. — [The Black Vault](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/stargate-collection-analysis-and-assessment-of-gateway-process-9-june-1983/)
- **[C] Missing page 25:** in a 2021 FOIA response the CIA said it could not locate page 25. A copy reportedly surfaced later via the Monroe Institute. — [The Black Vault](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/stargate-collection-analysis-and-assessment-of-gateway-process-9-june-1983/)
- **[A] Related Army–Monroe contract:** a CIA Reading Room memo records that the INSCOM Center Lane office gave Robert Monroe a personal services contract in July 1983 to test Hemi-Sync for "psychoenergetic" collection. A later memo describes four progressive training phases, ending in full use of Hemi-Sync during Center Lane sessions. — [CIA Reading Room print 271637](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/print/271637); [CIA Reading Room print 1521993](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/print/1521993)
- **[A] Other planned uses:** the same records link Hemi-Sync to a screening and managerial training program (RAPT) and to a proposal for INSCOM's CENTEX to use it for language learning. — [CIA Reading Room print 271637](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/print/271637)
- **[C] Recent attention:** heavy viral coverage since about 2021–2025, for example claims that "the CIA discovered how to manifest." This is commentary, not new documentation. — [ZeroHedge (example of commentary)](https://www.zerohedge.com/political/did-cia-discover-how-manifest-your-best-life)

### Inferences
- The Gateway report records that Army intelligence was *interested in* and *theorized about* binaural-beat audio. It does not show that binaural audio produced verified intelligence effects. It is an internal analysis, not a test with controls or measured results.

### Gaps
- I did not obtain the formal evaluation of the Hemi-Sync training results (whether it improved remote-viewer performance). No document showing a measured outcome was found.

## 3. Project STARGATE / GRILL FLAME / CENTER LANE records involving audio or binaural methods

### Takeaway
The audio component of the remote-viewing programs is documented mainly through the 1983–84 Monroe Institute Hemi-Sync training arrangement (section 2). The overall program ended in 1995 after a CIA-commissioned American Institutes for Research (AIR) review found no demonstrated operational value.

### Cited Findings
- **[C] Program lineage:** the 1977 GONDOLA WISH effort became GRILL FLAME at Fort Meade, was renamed INSCOM CENTER LANE in 1983, and later became SUN STREAK and then STARGATE. Sources disagree on whether formalization came in 1977 or 1978. — [Wikipedia, Stargate Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stargate_Project_(U.S._Army_unit))
- **[A] Hemi-Sync in Center Lane:** the July 1983 Monroe contract and phased Hemi-Sync training are documented (see section 2). — [CIA Reading Room print 271637](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/print/271637)
- **[A] Primary STARGATE documents are hosted in the CIA-RDP96-00788R series** (example: CIA-RDP96-00788R001700210002-0, the same box as the Gateway document). — [Black Vault mirror of CIA-RDP96-00788R001700210002-0](https://documents3.theblackvault.com/documents/stargate/pdfs/CIA-RDP96-00788R001700210002-0.pdf)
- **[B]/[C] Termination:** the program was ended and declassified in 1995 after the AIR review concluded that remote viewing had not been shown to work and had not been used operationally. — [Wikipedia, Stargate Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stargate_Project_(U.S._Army_unit))

### Gaps
- The number of trainees who went through Hemi-Sync, and any performance data, were not confirmed from primary text.

## 4. The "Moscow Signal": microwave, not sound

### Takeaway
From the 1950s to the 1970s the Soviets beamed low-intensity **microwave** radiation at the US Embassy in Moscow. It was not acoustic. The Pentagon's Project PANDORA, including the animal-exposure component BIZARRE, studied possible biological and behavioral effects and never established harm. The signal was largely attributed to eavesdropping or jamming purposes. It is often conflated with "sonic attacks."

### Cited Findings
- **[A] National Security Archive tranches:** declassified Moscow Signal documents released in three tranches. They cover Project BIZARRE (primate radiation experiments within PANDORA), the 1975 Kissinger and Ford diplomacy to stop the radiation, and radiation reports at Spaso House during Vice President Nixon's 1959 visit. — [NSA Archive document 06](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/documents/ri5s7t-pi5hv/06.pdf); [NSA Archive document 14](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/documents/rhwvuj-tzy4b/14.pdf)
- **[C] Intensity:** officials estimated the signal at about 5 microwatts per cm², below the level needed for heating. Pentagon researchers never established a link between the exposure and injury. The signal was ultimately attributed to eavesdropping rather than mind control. PANDORA ended about 1970. A 1977 DARPA letter gives its total cost as $4,615,000. — [AOL/Yahoo report on the NSArchive documents](https://www.aol.com/news/soviet-plot-bombard-u-embassy-090023543.html); [Foreign Policy, "The Secret History of Diplomats and Invisible Weapons" (2017)](https://foreignpolicy.com/2017/08/25/the-secret-history-of-diplomats-and-invisible-weapons-russia-cuba/)
- **[A] CIA Reading Room clipping** "'Brainwash' attempt by Russians?", showing contemporaneous speculation. — [CIA Reading Room node 408127](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/node/408127)
- **[C] Foreign Service Journal history (Nov. 2022).** — [AFSA FSJ Nov 2022](https://afsa.org/sites/default/files/flipping_book/1122/19)
- **[D]** Claims that ambassadors died from the radiation (for example in Lobster Magazine) are unsupported. — [Lobster Magazine](https://www.lobster-magazine.co.uk/?p=7666)

### Inferences
- The Moscow Signal is the main historical precedent for the *radiofrequency* hypothesis in Havana Syndrome, not for an *acoustic* one. Its link to perceived sound comes through the separate microwave auditory effect (the "Frey effect"); see section 6.

### Gaps
- I did not read the full findings of the 1970s State Department epidemiological study of Moscow embassy staff (the Lilienfeld/Johns Hopkins study).

## 5. Havana Syndrome / Anomalous Health Incidents (AHIs): acoustic vs. other explanations

### Takeaway
The "sonic attack" framing of 2017 did not survive scrutiny. The audio recordings were matched to crickets: by Stubbs & Montealegre-Zapata in 2019, and with high confidence by JASON in 2018, which also found the audio was not a by-product of RF or ultrasound. Expert bodies then moved to pulsed RF as the "most plausible" mechanism: the National Academies in 2020, and the IC Experts Panel in 2022, which also kept ultrasound open in limited close-range cases. The 2023 Intelligence Community Assessment and its December 2024 update judged a foreign adversary "very unlikely" responsible. The 2024 NIH studies found no MRI-detectable brain injury. In June 2026, DNI Gabbard **rescinded** the 2023 and 2025 ICAs for faulty tradecraft. As of the latest reporting found, no replacement assessment had been published.

### Cited Findings — chronology
- **[B] State Department Accountability Review Board (report dated June 2018; declassified Feb. 10, 2021, via an NSArchive FOIA):**
  - It found a department response marked by "a lack of senior leadership, ineffective communications, and systemic disorganization."
  - It found that the pattern of injuries "suggests a targeted action" but did not identify a cause.
  - It faulted the CIA for not sharing information about its officers' health in 2016–17.
  - It made 30 recommendations. The released copy is heavily redacted (SECRET//NOFORN, declassification date 2043).
  - The "sonic" label came from press coverage, not the ARB.
  - Confidence: High.
  - Sources: [Unredacted (NSArchive blog)](https://unredacted.com/2021/11/19/2021-documents-in-review-declassified-state-department-review-of-the-havana-syndrome/); [NSArchive OCR](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/media/20861/ocr); [CNN, Feb. 19, 2021](https://edition.cnn.com/2021/02/19/americas/us-cuba-biden-intl)
- **[B] GAO-18-615 (2018)** reviewed the State Department's response. — [GAO-18-615](https://gao.gov/assets/gao-18-615.pdf)
- **[B] JASON report on the Havana audio (completed Nov. 2018; released via a FOIA request by BuzzFeed News in 2021):**
  - The recorded sounds in at least 8 of 21 incidents were "most likely" the Indies short-tailed cricket. The cricket's call matches the spectral properties of the recordings "in nuanced detail."
  - With high confidence, the audio was **not** produced by nonlinear detection of high-power RF or ultrasound pulses.
  - Psychogenic effects may have played a role.
  - It kept a mechanical device or structure-borne vibration as plausible alternatives.
  - It did not explain the injuries.
  - Confidence: High.
  - Sources: [FAS/IRP copy of JASON "havana.pdf"](https://irp.fas.org/agency/dod/jason/havana.pdf); [BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/danvergano/havana-syndrome-jason-crickets); [National Interest](https://nationalinterest.org/node/194849)
- **[C] Stubbs & Montealegre-Zapata, bioRxiv preprint (Jan. 4, 2019; doi 10.1101/510834),** "Recording of 'sonic attacks' on U.S. diplomats in Cuba spectrally matches the echoing call of a Caribbean cricket":
  - The AP-released recording matches *Anurogryllus celerinictus* in duration, pulse repetition rate, power spectrum and the frequency decline within each pulse.
  - The match is closer when indoor echoes are accounted for.
  - The paper explicitly does not address the medical symptoms.
  - It was not peer-reviewed when it appeared.
  - Sources: [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/510834v1); [ScienceBlog](https://scienceblog.com/?p=505228); [Fox/LiveScience](https://www.foxnews.com/world/sonic-weapon-used-on-us-diplomats-in-cuba-may-have-been-crickets-scientists-say)
- **[B] National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine (Dec. 2020),** "An Assessment of Illness in U.S. Government Employees and Their Families at Overseas Embassies" (State Department-commissioned; nap.edu catalog 25889):
  - "Directed, pulsed radio frequency energy appears to be the most plausible mechanism" for the distinctive acute symptoms, such as head pressure, dizziness and cognitive difficulty.
  - The committee could not rule out other causes. It noted secondary psychological and social factors and did not name a source or call it an attack.
  - It did not endorse an acoustic mechanism.
  - Confidence: High.
  - Sources: [Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2020/12/05/world/report-points-microwave-attack-likely-source-mystery-illnesses-that-hit-diplomats-spies); [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/7504663/us-canada-diplomats-havana-syndrome-report)
- **[B] IC Experts Panel (ODNI/CIA), executive summary dated Feb. 1, 2022:**
  - The panel started from five candidate mechanisms, including acoustic signals and RF/electromagnetic energy. It reviewed more than 1,000 classified documents.
  - It concluded that "pulsed electromagnetic energy, particularly in the radiofrequency range, plausibly explains the core characteristics."
  - It found that ultrasound could also explain them, but only in limited cases (close range).
  - It judged psychosocial factors alone unable to account for the core characteristics in some cases.
  - It did not examine attribution.
  - A later declassified version reportedly names parametric acoustic arrays (directional loudspeakers) as the most plausible ultrasound technology.
  - Confidence: Medium-High. I could not open the PDF; this is from ODNI page text and reporting.
  - Sources: [ODNI executive summary PDF](https://www.dni.gov/files/ODNI/documents/assessments/2022%5F02%5F01%5FAHI%5FExecutive%5FSummary%5FFINAL%5FRedacted.pdf); [ODNI, "Complementary Efforts on AHIs"](https://www.dni.gov/index.php/newsroom/reports-publications/reports-publications-2022/3591-complementary-efforts-on-anomalous-health-incidents); [UPI, Feb. 2, 2022](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/US/2022/02/02/Expert-panel-says-electromagnetic-energy-plausible-cause-for-Havana-Syndrome/6111643839637/); [Just Security](https://www.justsecurity.org/105584/questions-havana-syndrome-from-here/)
- **[B] ODNI Intelligence Community Assessment (March 1, 2023):**
  - Most IC elements judged a foreign adversary "very unlikely" to be responsible: two with moderate-to-high confidence and three with moderate confidence. Two others judged it "unlikely," with low confidence.
  - Symptoms were judged probably the result of preexisting conditions, conventional illness and environmental factors.
  - Sources: [DNI statement on the ICA](https://www.dni.gov/index.php/newsroom/press-releases/press-releases-2023/3674-intelligence-community-assessment-on-anomalous-health-incidents); [AOL/AP](https://www.aol.com/most-u-spy-agencies-think-161844981.html)
- **[A]/[C] NIH studies, JAMA, March 18, 2024:**
  - **Pierpaoli et al. (MRI):** 81 AHI participants vs. 48 controls. No significant differences on any MRI measure, with scans from as early as 14 days after an incident.
  - **Chan et al. (clinical):** 86 participants vs. 30 controls. Most auditory, vestibular, cognitive, visual and blood-biomarker tests showed no differences. Participants had higher fatigue, PTSD, depression and imbalance symptoms.
  - Pierpaoli said the null result "does not exclude that an adverse event impacting the brain occurred."
  - The June 2026 ODNI rescission memo reportedly called an "ethically flawed medical study" a basis of the ICAs. Which study was meant is not confirmed.
  - Sources: [Applied Radiology](https://appliedradiology.com/Articles/no-mri-detectable-brain-injury-found-in-people-with-havana-syndrome); [Healio](https://www.healio.com/news/neurology/20240322/havana-syndrome-not-correlated-with-results-of-mribased-biomarkers-neuropsych-tests); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/3/18/no-evidence-of-brain-injury-in-people-suffering-havana-syndrome-us-study)
- **[B] House Intelligence Committee interim report (Dec. 2024, led by Rep. Rick Crawford):**
  - The 2023 ICA "lacked analytic integrity" and was "highly irregular."
  - A foreign adversary is increasingly likely behind some cases.
  - This is a contested congressional view, not an IC finding.
  - Sources: [House Intelligence Committee, Unclassified AHI Report](https://intelligence.house.gov/UploadedFiles/Unclassified_AHI_Report.pdf); [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/havana-syndrome-intelligence-report-questioned-house-committee-60-minutes/)
- **[B] Updated ICA (as of Dec. 2024; released Jan. 2025):**
  - Five of seven IC elements still judged it "very unlikely" that a foreign actor has a capability such as a pulsed RF weapon, or is responsible.
  - Two elements shifted. One now sees a "roughly even chance" that a foreign actor used a novel weapon against a small number of people. The other sees a roughly even chance that such a weapon was developed but probably not deployed. Both views are held with low confidence.
  - The shift concerns RF/directed energy, not acoustics.
  - Sources: [ODNI updated assessment PDF](https://archive.dni.gov/files/ODNI/documents/assessments/NIC-Unclassified-ICA-Updated-Assessment-AHI-December2024.pdf); [FAS/IRP copy](https://irp.fas.org/nic/ahi-updated.pdf); [Sen. Warner statement](https://www.warner.senate.gov/public/index.cfm/2025/1/sen-warner-on-anomalous-health-incidents-ahi-update); [JEIC summary](https://www.jeic-emf.jp/english/academic/info/20250120.html)
- **[B] FY2026 NDAA (signed Dec. 2025)** requires the DNI to set standard guidelines for reporting AHIs. — [CNN, June 12, 2026](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/12/politics/gabbard-rescinds-biden-era-intel-assessments-that-were-skeptical-about-havana-syndrome)
- **[B] ODNI rescission (June 12, 2026):**
  - DNI Tulsi Gabbard rescinded the March 2023 ICA and the Jan. 2025 update.
  - A memo to the IC said the assessments selectively excluded relevant intelligence, suppressed alternative analysis and relied on an "ethically flawed medical study."
  - Chairman Crawford called the assessments "flawed, fraudulent, and manufactured."
  - Gabbard was due to leave office later that month. Whether a new assessment will follow is unclear.
  - Confidence: High that the rescission occurred (multiple outlets). Its content comes from the memo as reported, not from the memo itself.
  - Sources: [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/12/politics/gabbard-rescinds-biden-era-intel-assessments-that-were-skeptical-about-havana-syndrome); [AP via Baltimore Sun](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2026/06/12/gabbard-rescinds-intelligence-assessments-on-mysterious-havana-syndrome/); [Washington Times](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jun/12/dni-rebukes-faulty-intelligence-analyses-havana-syndrome/)
- **[B] ODNI Annual Threat Assessment 2026** (exists; I did not confirm whether it mentions AHIs). — [ATA 2026 PDF](https://archive.dni.gov/files/ODNI/documents/assessments/ATA-2026-Unclassified-Report.pdf)
- **[C]/[D] Commentary arguing Russian responsibility (2026)**, for example RealClearDefense, Jan. 23, 2026. This is opinion, not an assessment. — [RealClearDefense](https://www.realcleardefense.com/information-warfare/articles/russia-behind-havana-syndrome-2026-01-23)

### Inferences
- Across official bodies, the *acoustic* hypothesis in its original form, audible sound as the injuring agent, has effectively been dropped:
  - The recordings point to crickets (JASON, Stubbs).
  - Audible sound at plausible levels was not considered capable of the injuries.
  - The remaining "acoustic" thread is *ultrasound at close range* (IC Experts Panel, 2022).
- The live official debate is RF/directed energy versus non-attack explanations, and since June 2026 it has been politically unsettled, with no current standing ICA.

### Gaps
- No replacement ODNI assessment after the June 2026 rescission was found. I could not confirm events from July to October 2026.
- I did not fetch the 2018 JAMA study of Havana patients (Swanson et al., University of Pennsylvania) or the critiques of it.
- I did not verify the full text of the 2022 Experts Panel or the 2024 ICA from primary PDFs because of DNS failures. Wording comes from secondary quotes.

## 6. Declassified Soviet/Russian, East German (Stasi) and other foreign acoustic research in Western archives

### Takeaway
Western archives hold mostly second-hand US intelligence characterizations of Soviet and Warsaw Pact "psychotronic," microwave-auditory and infrasonic research from the 1970s. These are DIA reports and press clippings preserved in the CIA Reading Room. I found no primary Stasi acoustic-weapon documents in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- **[A]/[C] CIA Reading Room clippings** of a Reuters story on a DIA report (supplied to Reuters by a private source). The report warned that Soviet and Czech "psychotronic" research "has powerful potential for use as an effective weapon against groups of men and leaders." The story noted that skeptics called such reports "disinformation." — [CIA Reading Room print 306575](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/print/306575); [CIA Reading Room print 304727](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/print/304727)
- **[C] DIA report DST-1810S-387-75 (Sept. 1975)** on Soviet and Czech parapsychology. It is cited secondarily for listing "infrasonic means using acoustic radiation to affect biological targets." That phrase also appears in a 1977 disarmament-conference list of weapons. Secondary source only; Confidence: Low-Medium. — [Laetus in Praesens 1978 newsletter PDF](https://www.laetusinpraesens.org/pdfs/1970s/1978_6,7.pdf)
- **[C]/[D] A 1976 DIA warning** reportedly said Soviet microwave research on "internal sound perception" (the microwave auditory effect) could be used for "disrupting the behavior patterns of military or diplomatic personnel." It is widely quoted in Havana-era commentary; I did not verify it against the primary document. — [RealClearDefense (2026)](https://www.realcleardefense.com/information-warfare/articles/russia-behind-havana-syndrome-2026-01-23); [CIA Reading Room node 1899339](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/node/1899339)

## 7. Interrogation techniques using loud music and noise (SSCI 2014 report)

### Takeaway
The Senate Select Committee on Intelligence's December 2014 Executive Summary documents loud noise and music used with darkness, shackling and sleep deprivation, notably at the CIA site code-named COBALT. Its Finding #1 states that the enhanced techniques were not an effective means of obtaining intelligence or cooperation.

### Cited Findings
- **[B] Report scope:** a 524-page public Executive Summary of a roughly 6,700-page classified study, released Dec. 9, 2014. — [SSCI Findings and Conclusions PDF](https://www.intelligence.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/sites-default-files-press-findings-and-conclusions.pdf); [Internet Archive full summary](https://archive.org/details/sscistudy1/mode/2up)
- **[B] COBALT:** detainees were held in complete darkness, constantly shackled in isolated cells "with loud noise or music and only a bucket to use for human waste." The chief of interrogations described the site as a "dungeon." — [SSCI Findings and Conclusions](https://www.intelligence.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/sites-default-files-press-findings-and-conclusions.pdf); [Reuters graphic summary](https://graphics.thomsonreuters.com/14/CIA-detainees/index.html)
- **[B] Sleep deprivation:**
  - It lasted up to 180 hours, usually standing or in stress positions, sometimes with hands shackled overhead.
  - At least five detainees had disturbing hallucinations during prolonged sleep deprivation.
  - In at least two of those cases the CIA continued anyway.
  - Source: [SSCI Findings and Conclusions](https://www.intelligence.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/sites-default-files-press-findings-and-conclusions.pdf)
- **[B] Finding #1:** "The CIA's use of its enhanced interrogation techniques was not an effective means of acquiring intelligence or gaining cooperation from detainees." — [SSCI Findings and Conclusions](https://www.intelligence.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/sites-default-files-press-findings-and-conclusions.pdf)
- **[B]/[C] Dispute:** CIA Director John Brennan acknowledged "shortcomings" and mistakes but disputed the conclusions on effectiveness. — [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/us/cia-mistreated-detainees-misrepresented-intelligence-mismanaged-program-senate-probe-finds.amp); [Salon](https://salon.com/2014/12/09/10_appalling_findings_in_the_senates_torture_report)

### Inferences
- The best-documented use of sound on people by a US intelligence agency is not frequency-specific. It is high-volume noise and music used as an environmental stressor and to prevent sleep, combined with other techniques.

### Gaps
